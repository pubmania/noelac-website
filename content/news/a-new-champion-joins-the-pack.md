## Remrah High Flyer at Culversapso JW

**BIRMINGHAM — May 7, 2026**

The North of England Lhasa Apso Club is howling with pride following a historic performance at the **Birmingham National Championship Show** yesterday.

We are thrilled to announce that the breed has a new Champion: **Ch. Remrah High Flyer at Culversapso JW**, owned by our very own committee member, **Dianne Culverhouse**. 

{{< cloudinary-img src="2026_Birmingham_National_Show_Champion_Flyer" caption="Flyer is the Winner of the 2026 Birmingham National Show" align="right" >}}

### **A Day of Double Victory**
"Flyer" did not stop at securing his crown. In a spectacular display of longevity and breed type, he also went on to be awarded **Veteran Group 4** on the day, proving that a true Lhasa Apso carries its dignity and "Sentinel" spirit well into its senior years. 

### **The Lineage of a Legend**
Bred by **Kellie Tombleson**, Flyer’s success is a testament to careful breeding and dedication to the standard. The celebration was a true family affair for the breed; Flyer was joined in the ring by **Sarah Hattrell**, owner of Flyer’s sire, **Ch. Khinjan Scarlett Pimpernel** (pictured on the left).

### **Notes from the Monastery**
This achievement will be permanently recorded in our digital archives at **noelac.org**. Seeing a veteran take a Group placing while securing a Championship title is a rare and inspiring moment for all enthusiasts.

Huge congratulations to Dianne and the entire team behind Flyer!
